## Your account at a glance

The first card, **Account**, shows:

- The email you sign in with, your plan, and when you joined.
- **Requests used** this month against your allowance, and when it resets.
- Your projects, your rate limit and any request packs.
- Your **AI credits**, each grant with what is left and when it expires.
- **Theme**: light or dark. The choice also applies on the Stubbase website.

## Change your name

The **Your name** card sets the name shown in the account menu.

## Change your password

In the **Password** card, enter your current password and a new one. You stay
signed in on this device and are signed out everywhere else.

- The current password is always asked for, so someone at your unlocked
  computer can't lock you out.
- Forgotten it? Choose **Use an email code instead** and a 6-digit code is
  emailed to you.
- Signed up with Google or GitHub? You have no password yet, so the card says
  so and offers **Send code**: you set your first password with the 6-digit
  code it emails you.

## Forgot your password while signed out

On the login page, choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and type the
6-digit code you receive with a new password. The email also has a link that
fills the code in for you. Resetting signs you out everywhere else.

A code lasts 15 minutes and works once, and each address gets at most five
reset emails an hour.

## Delete your account

The **Delete account** card at the bottom removes your Stubbase account and
signs you out on every device. It can't be undone.

**1. Delete your projects first.** The card lists them, each with its own
delete button, and a live project has to be stopped first.

**2. Enter your password.** Signed up with Google or GitHub? Set a password in
the Password card first.

Your name, email and password are removed, and the address is free at once.
Signing up with it again starts a clean account on the Free plan. One thing
carries over: requests already used that calendar month still count, so
deleting and signing up again doesn't reset your allowance.
