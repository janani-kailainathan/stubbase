## What a reply costs

One credit is 1,000 tokens the AI model read and wrote. A reply is charged once,
when it ends, for every step it took, rounded up. Designing a small table costs
a few credits; seeding hundreds of rows costs more.

After each reply a toast shows what it used and how many are left. Your balance
is also in the Usage panel at the bottom of the APIs rail, and on your account
card under Settings.

## Failed replies are free

If the AI model is busy or something goes wrong, you're told the reply wasn't
charged, and nothing is taken. When the model is only briefly busy, the
Co-Pilot waits a moment and tries again before giving up.

## Where credits come from

| Source | Credits | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| **Gift**, every new account | 1,000 | 3 months from sign-up |
| **Pro plan** | 5,000 every month | Until the end of that month |
| **Credit packs**, any plan | 5,000, 20,000 or 60,000 | 12 months |

The gift comes once per email inbox. Deleting your account and signing up
again doesn't give you another, and neither does adding a `+tag` to your
address. Credit packs aren't on sale yet.

## Which credits are used first

The ones that expire soonest, so this month's Pro credits and your gift go
before a pack you paid for. Your account card lists each grant in the order it
will be used, with what is left and when it expires.

## Running out

When you have none left, the chat box reads **Out of AI credits** and stays
disabled, with a note on how to get more. Your API is not affected: it keeps
serving as before.

A reply that costs more than you have left stops your balance at zero, never
below, so the next grant isn't eaten by a debt.

## One reply at a time

The Co-Pilot answers one message at a time per account, so wait for a reply
before sending the next.
